## 5. A patio on a sloped yard

If your yard slopes, a [retaining wall](https://alphahardscapes.com/retaining-walls-connecticut/) can create a flat area for the patio. Plan the wall and the patio together.

## 6. A patio with stone steps

Where the patio sits above or below the house, [stone steps](https://alphahardscapes.com/stone-steps-connecticut/) make the way down easy and look finished.

## Which idea fits my yard?

Think about three things: how many people you host, whether the yard slopes, and whether you want cooking, a fire, or both. Connecticut ground freezes and thaws every winter, so ask any contractor how the base under the patio is built. Our guide to [how long a paver patio lasts](https://alphahardscapes.com/uncategorized/how-long-does-a-paver-patio-last/) explains why the base matters.
