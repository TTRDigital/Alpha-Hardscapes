## What does an outdoor kitchen need first?

A firm, level surface. In Connecticut the ground freezes and thaws every winter, so the base under a patio matters as much as what you see on top. That is why we plan the kitchen and the patio as one project. Our guide to a [patio around an outdoor kitchen](https://alphahardscapes.com/uncategorized/patio-around-outdoor-kitchen-ct/) shows how the two fit together.

## Where should the kitchen go?

Most homeowners put it close to the back door, with room to walk around the cooking area and a separate spot to sit. Keep the grill side and the seating side apart so people are not in the cook's way. If you also want a fire pit, plan its spot at the same time: see our [fire features](https://alphahardscapes.com/fire-features-connecticut/).
