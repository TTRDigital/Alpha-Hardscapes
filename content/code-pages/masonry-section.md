## Masonry or hardscaping: what is the difference?

Hardscaping is the stone and paver work in your yard, such as patios, walks and walls. Masonry is the craft behind it: setting stone and block so it stays level and tight. We do both, so one contractor can handle a yard from the steps to the patio.
